How Glass Box works
Not AI magic.
AI math — audited.
Every number you see has been fetched from an institutional API, verified by an independent agent, and cleared before display. No guess work. No hallucinations on screen.
01 — FETCH
⬇
Agent A1 pulls institutional data
Debt-to-equity, Altman Z-Score, free cash flow, 30 years of history — directly from Financial Modeling Prep's institutional API. Twelve Data provides real-time technicals. No aggregators, no intermediaries.
A1 Data Fetcher · A2 Technical Analyzer · A3 Sentiment Scanner
02 — EXPLAIN
✎
Agent A5 translates to plain English
Claude Sonnet 4.6 generates a clear narrative from 12 risk metrics. Every number in the text hyperlinks to its raw data source. Every claim is traceable. The narrative is written, not hallucinated.
A4 Risk Calculator · A5 Narrative Generator
03 — AUDIT
✓
Agent A6 audits before you see it
The Auditor cross-checks A5's narrative against raw API JSON. If anything doesn't match, it is flagged and corrected silently. You only see verified output. We publish the error rate publicly, every week.
A6 Auditor · A7 Compliance Monitor

Common questions
FAQ
Is this investment advice?
No. Glass Box is a financial research and data verification tool. It provides risk analysis based on institutional data. It does not tell you to buy or sell any security. Always consult a licensed financial professional before making investment decisions.
What is the Discrepancy Rate?
It is the percentage of AI-generated statements that our Auditor Agent (A6) finds inconsistent with the raw API data. We publish this publicly every week. A lower rate means our AI is more accurate. This week's rate is 0.8%.
What data sources does Glass Box use?
Financial Modeling Prep (FMP) for fundamental data including Altman Z-Score, D/E ratio, free cash flow, and 30+ years of history. Twelve Data for real-time technicals including Beta, RSI, and volume. SEC EDGAR (free) for sentiment from 10-K/10-Q filings.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. There are no lock-in contracts, no annual commitments unless you choose the annual plan, and no cancellation fees. Cancel from your account settings in 10 seconds. Your data is retained for 30 days after cancellation.
